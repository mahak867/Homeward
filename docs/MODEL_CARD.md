# Model card: HomeWard 24 h deterioration early warning

**Task:** estimate P(NEWS2 ≥ 5, sustained over two readings, within the next 24 h) for a home-monitored patient whose NEWS2 is currently < 5.

**Model:** L2-regularised logistic regression (numpy, Newton/IRLS) on 18 standardised features. Coefficients are **sign-constrained to physiology**: a feature whose fitted sign contradicted the expected direction was dropped and the model refit. Dropped in the current model: `slope6_hr, slope6_temp, slope12_hr, slope12_temp, note_concern`. They were collinear with the level-deviation features, or uninformative in simulation.

**Explanations:** exact additive contributions `coef_i × z_i`, shown as plain-language sentences. At most one line per vital sign.

**Data:** 900 synthetic patients × 96 h at 5-minute sampling, generated by `homeward/physiology.py`:
- 25% COPD, with Scale-2 targets and some on home oxygen;
- 45% stable, of whom 35% have a benign self-limiting upset as a confounder;
- deteriorations: sepsis-like, heart-failure decompensation, COPD exacerbation and a sudden acute hypoxia event that cannot be predicted;
- random sensor artifacts (removed by the quality gate), random data gaps, and simulated caregiver notes.

**Split:** patient-level 70/30 (no patient appears in both sets). Samples are taken hourly from hour 30 (24 h baseline + 6 h trend) until the event.

**Threshold:** the lowest threshold whose false early-warning rate on stable *training* patients is ≤ 0.15 per patient-day, using the same "sustained 1 h" rule as the live system. Current value: 0.39.

## Held-out results (synthetic)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Sample-level AUROC | 0.754 |
| Deteriorations flagged before NEWS2 ≥ 5 (within 48 h) | 106/144 |
| Median lead time, AI | 10.2 h |
| Median lead time, NEWS2 ≥ 3 baseline | 6.0 h |
| False early-warnings per stable patient-day, AI | 0.115 |
| Alerts per stable patient-day, NEWS2 ≥ 3 | 0.265 |
| Sepsis-like flagged / median lead | 51/54 · 7.7 h |
| Heart failure flagged / median lead | 41/41 · 15.9 h |
| COPD exacerbation flagged / median lead | 7/8 · 15.3 h |
| Acute hypoxia (sudden) flagged | 6/38 (expected: not forecastable) |

Exact numbers are in `docs/model_metrics.json`. Regenerate everything with `python -m homeward.train` (about 60 s).

## Intended use and limits

- Decision support for a care team, **in addition to** NEWS2 and never instead of it. The engine never lets the model lower a NEWS2-driven tier.
- **Not clinically validated.** All performance numbers describe a simulator. Real deployment would need prospective validation, calibration on real data, bias analysis across age, sex and comorbidity, and regulatory review.
- Not suitable for sudden events (arrhythmia, embolism, falls). Those are handled by real-time NEWS2 confirmation, red-flag notes and device alerts.
