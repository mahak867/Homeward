🧪 Resilience Lab - inject real faults into the running system and watch it cope

Internet outage (all homes)Hubs keep recording locally, sound an in-room alarm for critical patients, and replay everything on reconnect.
Duplicate packet stormEvery upload is sent twice. Ingestion is idempotent on (device, sequence).
Late / out-of-order deliveryReadings are held back and shuffled. Analysis keys on measurement time, not arrival.
AI service crashCircuit breaker opens; transparent rule fallback takes over. The NEWS2 safety floor never stops.
Power cut (all homes)Hubs switch to battery and keep monitoring; oxygen concentrators stop, so oxygen-dependent patients get an immediate equipment alert.
Bedside hub failure Status turns UNKNOWN (never "stable" on old data), then a device-offline alert escalates.
